# 🔏 Data Privacy and Retention

## Purpose
The workbook's fields and sensitivity classification have not been inspected. This guide does not assert that the dataset is anonymous or legally cleared for public redistribution.

## Current project context
Classify each field, minimize identifiers, document source authority and intended use, restrict exports, and define retention with the data owner. Review cached or exported data in PBIX, Excel, PDF, CSV, and screenshots before sharing.

## Recommended workflow
Retention periods and legal basis must come from the owner and applicable policy. Do not invent a period or compliance claim.
