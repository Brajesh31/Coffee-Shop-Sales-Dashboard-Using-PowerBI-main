# 👥 User Personas

## Purpose
Primary persona, analyst: needs to compare periods, locations, product groups, and time patterns; cares about definitions, filter context, and reconciliation. Secondary persona, operations reviewer: wants a clear view of historical activity and potential demand concentration. Maintainer: needs to refresh, inspect, validate, and distribute a controlled PBIX revision.

## Current project context
These are task-based design personas, not researched individuals or claims about actual repository users.

## Recommended workflow
Test whether each persona can answer a concrete question, understand the date range, identify active filters, interpret KPI definitions, and reproduce a surprising result. Record feedback before treating a persona as validated.

## Evidence and verification
Do not infer customer demographics or organizational roles from the workbook. User research should be documented separately if performed.
